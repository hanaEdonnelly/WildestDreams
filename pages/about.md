Wildest Dreams is Charleston's unofficial Taylor Swift tribute band. We bring high-energy entertainment with a diverse mix of all eras of Taylor's music that audiences know all too well. Our setlist transports audience members back to their front-row seat at the Eras tour, a first dance with their "Lover," and summer nights listening to "Our Song." 

Wildest Dreams brings together five professional musicians to recreate the energy and emotion of every Taylor Swift era. Featuring Sophia El-Zahr (vocals, piano), Lee Archer (guitar), Kristen Stewart (drums), Meredith Keough (violin), and Hana Donnelly (bass), the band delivers enchanted performances. 

Our members are professional and strive to make working with us smooth and stress-free. Let us help you make the event of your wildest dreams... are you ready for it?
